Before you come

A Few House Notes

Everything is cooked when it is ordered, so a full table takes a little longer than you might expect. Swallow and soups come out fastest; a whole roast chicken is forty minutes whatever we do.

Children are welcome and we have two high chairs. We take cash and transfer, and cards when the terminal behaves. There is no service charge.
