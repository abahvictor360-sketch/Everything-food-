Our experience

Our Stories Have Adventures.

Everything Food started in 2014 as one pot of jollof sold from a folding table on Aminu Kano Crescent. The table is gone. The pot is bigger. The recipe never changed, and neither did the rule that nothing leaves the kitchen we would not serve our own mother.

Three cooks became fourteen. We still buy pepper at Wuse Market on Tuesday mornings, and we still pound yam by hand because the machine gets it wrong.
