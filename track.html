Order status

Where Is My Food?

Put in the reference from your confirmation and we will pull it up. Most orders are at your door inside 45 minutes; the kitchen stage is the one that varies.

Look up an order

Your reference looks like EF-4821 and is in the confirmation message. We check it against the kitchen board and reply on WhatsApp, which is where our riders report in.

Or go straight to a person:

WhatsApp us Call the kitchen

If it is late

Past 45 minutes inside Wuse, Maitama or Garki and the delivery is on us — we take it off without being asked. Traffic on a Friday is the usual reason, and the rider will tell you where they are.

If it arrived wrong

Cold, missing a side, or not what you ordered: message us the same day with the reference and a photo. We remake it or refund it. We do not argue about ₦2,000 of rice.

What happens

Five Stages, Every Order

So you know what your reference means when we quote a stage back at you.

  1. 01

    Order received

    We have your order and payment is confirmed. You will get a reference like EF-4821.

    Straight away
  2. 02

    In the kitchen

    Your pot goes on. This is the longest stage and the one we will not rush.

    15 – 30 min
  3. 03

    Packed

    Plated, sealed, and into an insulated bag with the sides and cutlery.

    5 min
  4. 04

    With a rider

    On the road. This is when we can tell you roughly how far out they are.

    10 – 25 min
  5. 05

    Delivered

    Handed over. If anything arrived cold or wrong, tell us the same day.
